# Image Guide for Claude Medium Articles

## Overview
Medium articles perform significantly better with strong visuals. Each article in this series has specific image prompts. Here's how to create them.

---

## Free Image Generation Tools

### Option 1: Adobe Firefly (Recommended — Free tier available)
- Go to firefly.adobe.com
- Use the exact prompts below
- Download at 2048x1152px (16:9) for hero images

### Option 2: Midjourney
- Best quality for photorealistic and cinematic images
- Use prompts below with `--ar 16:9 --v 6` appended

### Option 3: DALL-E (via ChatGPT)
- Free with ChatGPT account
- Works well for infographics and simpler visuals

### Option 4: Unsplash / Pexels (No generation needed)
- Search terms below for each article
- Free, no attribution required for Unsplash

---

## Image Specs for Medium

| Use | Size | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / Feature image | 1400 x 800px min | 7:4 |
| In-article wide image | 1200 x 675px | 16:9 |
| Portrait / tall image | 800 x 1200px | 2:3 |

## Medium Image Tips

1. **Always add alt text** — it helps accessibility and SEO
2. **Caption every image** — adds context and looks professional
3. **Don't use images with text** unless it's a screenshot — they won't render well on mobile
4. **Use odd numbers** — 1, 3, or 5 images per article performs better than even numbers
5. **Hero image matters most** — it's what shows on the feed preview; spend extra time on it
