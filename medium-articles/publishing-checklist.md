# Medium Publishing Checklist & Best Practices

Use this before publishing every article in the Claude series.

---

## Pre-Writing: Strategy

- [ ] Target keyword identified (use it in title, subtitle, first paragraph)
- [ ] Audience defined (beginner / intermediate / developer / business)
- [ ] Article goal clear (educate / convert / entertain / inspire)
- [ ] Estimated read time planned (5–9 min is the Medium sweet spot)

---

## Writing: Structure Best Practices

### Title Formula
Medium titles that perform best follow one of these patterns:
- `[Number] [Topic] That Will [Outcome]` — *"10 Claude Prompts That Will Change How You Work"*
- `[Topic]: [Specific Promise]` — *"Claude AI: The Beginner's Guide to Your Most Powerful Thinking Partner"*
- `[Question format]` — *"Is Claude Better Than ChatGPT? An Honest Answer"*
- `How [Audience] Are Using [Topic] to [Result]` — *"How Developers Are Using Claude to 10x Their Productivity"*

### Subtitle
- Always add a subtitle in Medium (the italicized line under the title)
- Should expand on the title's promise
- 1 sentence max

### Opening Hook (First 3 Lines)
- **Most critical part of the article** — visible in feed previews
- Lead with a bold claim, surprising fact, or common misconception
- Never start with "In this article I will..."

### Structure Template
```
Hook (3 lines max)
Brief intro / framing (1 paragraph)
---
Section 1 (H2 heading + content)
Section 2 (H2 heading + content)
...
---
Summary / Call to Action
Follow prompt
```

---

## Formatting: What Works on Medium

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short paragraphs (2–4 lines) | Long walls of text |
| Bold key phrases | Over-bold everything |
| Subheadings every 200–300 words | Long sections without breaks |
| Pull quotes (the `>` blockquote) | Dense academic prose |
| Code blocks for any code | Inline unformatted code |
| Tables for comparisons | Bullet lists for everything |

## Tags (Choose Exactly 5)

Medium allows 5 tags. These perform well for Claude/AI content:

**High-traffic tags:**
- `Artificial Intelligence`
- `Technology`
- `Productivity`

**Niche (better for discovery):**
- `Claude`
- `AI Tools`
- `Prompt Engineering`
- `ChatGPT` (great for comparison articles)
- `Software Development` (for developer articles)
- `Machine Learning`

**Recommended combo per article:**
- Article 1 (Beginners): `Artificial Intelligence` · `Technology` · `Claude` · `AI Tools` · `Productivity`
- Article 2 (Prompting): `Artificial Intelligence` · `Prompt Engineering` · `Claude` · `AI Tools` · `Productivity`
- Article 3 (vs ChatGPT): `Artificial Intelligence` · `ChatGPT` · `Claude` · `Technology` · `AI Tools`
- Article 4 (Developers): `Software Development` · `Artificial Intelligence` · `Claude` · `Productivity` · `AI Tools`

## Publication Strategy

### Option A: Publish on Your Own Profile
- Full control
- Slower growth initially
- Good if you're building your own brand

### Option B: Submit to a Medium Publication
Best publications for AI/tech content:
- **Towards Data Science** (large audience, high standards)
- **Better Programming** (developer-focused)
- **The Startup** (broad tech/business)
- **AI Advances** (AI-specific)
- **Level Up Coding** (developer content)

To submit: Write the article, then use the "..." menu → "Add to publication"

### Recommended Order to Publish
1. Article 1 (Beginners Guide) — broadest audience, best for first article
2. Article 3 (Claude vs ChatGPT) — high search volume, controversial = shares
3. Article 2 (Prompting Best Practices) — practical, high save rate
4. Article 4 (Developers) — niche but very engaged audience

## Post-Publishing Checklist

- [ ] Share on LinkedIn with a 2-sentence hook + article link
- [ ] Share on Twitter/X with the best one-liner from the article
- [ ] Post in relevant Reddit communities (r/artificial, r/ChatGPT, r/MachineLearning — check rules first)
- [ ] Add to your Medium Reading List
- [ ] Respond to every comment within 24 hours (Medium algorithm rewards engagement)
- [ ] After 48 hours, check stats — if views are low, update the title

---

## Growth Tips Specific to AI Content

1. **Post on Thursdays or Tuesdays** — highest Medium readership
2. **Update articles** — Medium resurfaces updated articles in feeds
3. **Cross-post to Substack** — builds email list alongside Medium following
4. **Interlink your articles** — mention and link to your other pieces naturally
5. **Respond to comments publicly** — visible responses attract more readers
