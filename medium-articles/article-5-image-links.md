## Quick Customization Guide

For the **hero image** (appears at top of Medium article):
1. Open Option 4 (Blue Gradient LinkedIn Cover)
2. Replace the title text with: "10 Skills to Build a Design System"
3. Add subtitle: "From tokens to adoption — the complete guide"
4. Change background if desired to `#0A0E1A`
5. Export → download → upload to Medium as feature image

For the **mid-article infographic** (illustrates the 10 skills):
1. Open Option 1 (Hexagon Mind Map)
2. Label each hexagon with one of the 10 skills
3. Center: "Design System"
4. Export and insert into article after the "How the 10 Skills Fit Together" section
