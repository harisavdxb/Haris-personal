## Posting Tips

- Post Option A on a **Tuesday or Thursday morning** (8–10am your timezone)
- Add the article link as the **first comment** (not in the post body — LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links)
- Reply to every comment in the first hour — it dramatically increases reach
- Use the article's hero image as the post image
- Tag 1–2 people whose opinion you respect and ask what they'd add
