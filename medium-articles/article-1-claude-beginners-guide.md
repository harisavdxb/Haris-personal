## 5 Things Claude Does Better Than You'd Expect

### 1. Long-Document Analysis
Upload a 300-page contract, a research paper, or a financial report. Ask Claude to summarize, find contradictions, or pull specific data. It handles it without losing context.

### 2. Writing That Sounds Like a Human
Claude's writing doesn't have that robotic AI texture. It adapts tone — casual, formal, persuasive, technical — based on exactly what you ask for.

### 3. Code Debugging and Explanation
Paste broken code. Get not just a fix, but a clear explanation of WHY it was broken and HOW the fix works. Perfect for learning and for shipping.

### 4. Structured Thinking
Ask Claude to think through a business problem, a strategy, or a personal decision step by step. The reasoning is transparent, logical, and easy to follow.

### 5. Saying "I Don't Know"
Unlike some AI systems that confidently hallucinate, Claude is trained to admit uncertainty. This makes it dramatically more trustworthy for research and professional work.

## How to Get Started in Under 5 Minutes

1. Go to **claude.ai**
2. Create a free account
3. Start with this prompt:

```
"I'm [your role]. Help me [your goal]. Here's the context: [paste your context]. 
Give me a structured, step-by-step response."
```

That's it. You're already using it better than 80% of users.

## The One Thing Most Beginners Miss

Claude gets dramatically better when you **give it a role**.

Instead of: *"Summarize this article."*

Try: *"You are a senior editor at The Economist. Summarize this article in 5 bullet points, focusing on implications for emerging markets."*

The difference in output quality is remarkable.
